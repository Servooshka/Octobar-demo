About the game

A bar that runs on you

Octobar is a barmen simulator set in the alternative 1920s, where you work as an octopus-barmen serving up drinks for monstrous customers and learning their stories while doing so. It is a puzzle-strategy mashup where you'll need to plan out your movements before acting upon them, hopefully spicing up a well established game loop of this genre

How to play

A shift behind the bar

Tutorial slides from the game.

Keyboard and mouse controls: D moves to the next spot behind the bar, W looks up, S looks down, under the bar. With the mouse, pick ingredients from the inventory and click the shaker.
