About the game

An octopus bartender serves monstrous patrons in an alternative 1920s.

Octobar is a puzzle-strategy mashup set in the alternative 1920s. You work as an octopus bartender, serving drinks to monstrous customers and learning their stories while you do so. You plan your movements before acting on them, and you keep an eye on each guest's patience.
