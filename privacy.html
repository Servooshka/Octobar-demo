Privacy policy

This site is about the game Octobar. This page describes what data the site processes and how to contact us.

What we do not collect

  • The site does not use cookies for tracking.
  • The site does not include analytics or advertising networks.
  • The site does not ask for registration, passwords or payment details.

What may be processed technically

  • Hosting. The site and the game download are hosted on Cloudflare. Cloudflare may keep request logs (for example, IP address and time) under its own policy.
  • Fonts. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Google may receive your IP address when a font is loaded.
  • External links. The download link leads to an external hosting service with its own privacy policy. Other links lead to third-party resources, whose data handling is governed by their own policies.

Data you send yourself

If you write to us by email, we use your address and the content of the message to reply. We do not share them with third parties or use them for mailings without your consent.

Children

The site does not collect data about children. If you believe a child has written to us and want the correspondence deleted, please contact us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of the page.

Privacy questions: servoo@octobar.dev.
